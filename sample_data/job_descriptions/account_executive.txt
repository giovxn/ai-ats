Account Executive, Mid-Market (Riyadh)

Brightdesk (a fictional company) makes scheduling and workforce software for hospitals and clinics. We're opening our first office in Riyadh and need an Account Executive who can build the Saudi market with us from day one.

You'll run the full sales cycle with mid-market healthcare groups, usually 50 to 500 employees: prospecting, discovery, demos, proposals, negotiation and close. You'll work with one SDR and a solutions engineer, and you'll report to our Head of Sales for the Middle East. Your target is around SAR 2.5M in new annual recurring revenue in your first full year.

We need someone with at least 3 years of closing experience in B2B SaaS, a track record of hitting quota, and the confidence to sell to hospital directors and operations leaders. You must be fluent in Arabic and English, and you should already be based in Riyadh or be willing to relocate there. You should know your way around a CRM; we use HubSpot.

It's a plus if you've sold into healthcare before, if you've been an early hire in a new market, or if you know MEDDICC or a similar qualification method.

On-target earnings are split 50/50 between base and commission, with no cap. We also offer health insurance, a car allowance and a yearly team offsite.
