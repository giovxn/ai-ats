Operations Manager – Dubai

At Saffron Crate (a fictional company) we deliver fresh meal kits to 40,000 homes across the UAE every week, and we are looking for an Operations Manager to run our Al Quoz fulfilment centre. You will lead a team of around 60 pickers, packers and drivers across two shifts, own our daily on-time delivery rate, and work with the procurement and customer teams to cut food waste.

The person we have in mind has spent at least five years in warehouse, logistics or food operations, including two or more years managing frontline teams of 30 people or more. You are comfortable reading a P&L and building a labour plan in Excel, and you will need a valid UAE driving licence because the role moves between our two sites. Experience with cold-chain or chilled food handling would be a real advantage, as would Lean or Six Sigma training, and Arabic or Hindi helps on the floor but is not essential. A degree is welcome but we care more about what you have run than what you have studied.

What we offer: a competitive salary of AED 28,000 to 34,000 a month, annual flights home, family health insurance and 30 days' leave.
