SENIOR ACCOUNTANT – Group Finance
Location: Abu Dhabi, UAE (on-site)
Company: Harbourline Logistics Group (fictional)

Harbourline is a regional logistics group with 14 entities across the UAE, Oman and Saudi Arabia. Our Group Finance team is looking for a Senior Accountant to lead the month-end close for five of our operating companies and support the annual audit.

Key responsibilities:
1. Own the month-end and year-end close for assigned entities, including accruals, prepayments and reconciliations.
2. Prepare monthly management accounts and variance analysis for the CFO.
3. Prepare IFRS financial statements and lead the relationship with external auditors.
4. Manage intercompany reconciliations and eliminations for consolidation.
5. Make sure UAE VAT and Corporate Tax filings are accurate and on time.
6. Supervise and coach two junior accountants.
7. Improve close processes and controls in our ERP (Oracle NetSuite).

Requirements:
• Bachelor's degree in Accounting or Finance
• Professional qualification: ACCA, CPA or CA (fully qualified) is required
• Minimum 6 years of post-qualification experience, with at least 2 years in a Big Four audit firm or a multi-entity group
• Strong knowledge of IFRS and group consolidation
• Hands-on experience with UAE VAT; knowledge of UAE Corporate Tax
• Advanced Excel (pivot tables, lookups, financial modelling)
• Experience with an ERP system; NetSuite is a strong plus

It would be a bonus if you have:
• Experience in logistics, shipping or supply chain
• Power BI or similar reporting tools
• Arabic speaker

Package: tax-free salary, annual bonus, housing allowance, medical insurance.
