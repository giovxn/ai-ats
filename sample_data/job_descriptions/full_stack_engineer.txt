Full-Stack Software Engineer
Tallyway (fictional company) · Dubai, UAE · Hybrid, 3 days in office

About Tallyway
Tallyway builds invoicing and cash-flow software for small businesses across the Gulf. We are 40 people, Series A, and our product handles over 2 million invoices a month.

The role
You'll join a team of six engineers and own features end to end: from the database schema to the React screen a shop owner taps on their phone. You'll work closely with product and design, ship every week, and help us keep the codebase simple as we grow.

What you'll do
- Build and ship product features across our React frontend and Python (FastAPI) backend
- Design PostgreSQL schemas and write efficient queries
- Review code and help set engineering standards
- Talk to customers with our product team to understand problems before solving them
- Keep our CI, tests and monitoring healthy

What we're looking for
- 4+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong TypeScript and React
- Solid backend experience in Python (FastAPI, Django or Flask)
- Comfortable with SQL and relational databases (PostgreSQL preferred)
- Experience with cloud infrastructure (AWS or GCP)
- Writes clear, tested code and communicates well in English

Nice to have
- Experience with fintech, payments or accounting software
- Arabic language skills
- Experience with Docker and Kubernetes
- Contributions to open-source projects

What we offer
- Competitive salary plus equity
- Annual flight home and 25 days of leave
- Health insurance for you and your family
- Learning budget of AED 5,000 per year
